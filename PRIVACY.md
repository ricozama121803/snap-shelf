# Snap Shelf Privacy Policy

Snap Shelf does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

All content you save (text, screenshots, images, notes, tags, folders, and page metadata such as URL, title, and favicon) is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. It never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Snap Shelf has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Its only network requests are fetching the images and favicons you explicitly choose to save from the pages you are viewing.

You can delete saved items at any time inside the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.
